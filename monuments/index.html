Power Plant and stages

  • stage 1 — 1–3 Heavy Fuses
  • stage 2 — 4–9 Heavy Fuses
  • stage 3 — 10–17 Heavy Fuses
  • stage 4 — 18+ Heavy Fuses

Loot rooms unlocked by the power grid

  • stage 1: Ferry Terminal, Harbor, Junkyard, Power Plant
  • stage 2: Airfield, Radtown, Sewer Branch, Train Yard, Water Treatment Plant
  • stage 3: Launch Site, Military Tunnels, Missile Silo

What else the power grid turns on

  • stage 1: supply drops twice as often (terminal in the airfield tower), freezer with food at Supermarkets, garage with a car lift at Oxum's, clean water stations around the map (start: a gear into the valve), roadside transformers (power for your base)
  • stage 2: green recyclers — 60%
  • stage 3: crude oil pumps at the Dome (switch on the rig)
  • stage 4: red recycler 75%, call a Chinook with a locked crate to the airfield, satellite crash terminal at Launch Site

Heavy Fuses go into the fuse boxes at the Power Plant; each lasts about 2 h 40 min (4× longer on servers with 10 players or fewer), then the stage drops.

Crates, scientists and page counts are approximate.

Checked 17.11.2026: Facepunch news (Power Trip, Livestock, Printed and Polished), Corrosion Hour, Rust Wiki.
