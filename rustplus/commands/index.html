Rust+ chat commands

Anyone in your team types a command in game chat — the bot answers right there, tagged [RustTrackerBro]. Rust+ must be connected by at least one teammate in the bot.

CommandWhat the bot answers
!pop[RustTrackerBro] Online 87, queue 4

Server online and queue — asked from the server right when you type it.

!time[RustTrackerBro] Time 18:40, night in ~12 min

In-game time and how long until night or morning.

!wipe[RustTrackerBro] Wiped 3 d 4 h ago · Next ~ in 4 d 6 h · Global wipe in 15 d 22 h

Time since the wipe, the next wipe if the server wipes every week or two, and the Global wipe.

!timer 12[RustTrackerBro] ⏱ Timer: 12 min — I'll remind 1 min before and at zero

A timer for the crate or anything else: !timer 12:30, !timer crate (15 min; already running — !timer crate 9), !timer heli, !timer bradley; !timer — active timers, !timer stop — reset the last one. Up to 3 per team, shared by everyone.

!decay stone 300[RustTrackerBro] stone 300 HP — falls in about 3 h 0 min, reminder 10 min before

Look at an enemy wall with a hammer, type its material and HP (and the grid, if you like: !decay hqm 1200 G12) — the bot reminds in chat and in the bot when it's about to fall. Needs 🔥 Bro+ for at least one player on the team.

!decay[RustTrackerBro] Without upkeep it decays in about: twig 1 h, wood 3 h, stone 5 h, sheet metal 8 h, armored 12 h

How long walls of each material decay without upkeep, and whether your Tool Cupboard is already empty.

!upkeep[RustTrackerBro] Cupboards: «Home» 31 h 0 min

Upkeep left on your team's Tool Cupboards (a Storage Monitor on the cupboard).

!craft c4[RustTrackerBro] Timed Explosive Charge x1: explosives 20, cloth 5, tech trash 2 · workbench 3 · 30 s

Craft cost, workbench and time; with gunpowder or explosives — a second line with raw materials (sulfur, charcoal, frags, fuel).

!recycle tech trash 5[RustTrackerBro] Recycle Tech Trash x5: …

What you get from a recycler and in a safe zone.

!raid stone wall 2[RustTrackerBro] Stone wall x2: C4 4 · rockets 8 · satchels 20 · explo ammo 364 · beancans 92

How many C4, rockets, satchels, explosive ammo or beancans — and a second line with the cheapest option in sulfur.

!crate[RustTrackerBro] Crates: G12, K7

Where the locked crates are now. Only on servers that share map markers.

!oil[RustTrackerBro] Rig activated 3 min ago

When the Oil Rig was triggered (a Chinook with scientists flies in). Only on servers that share map markers.

!bradley[RustTrackerBro] Bradley destroyed 4 min ago

When Bradley was destroyed. Only on servers that share map markers.

!cargo · !heli · !chinook · !vendor[RustTrackerBro] Cargo at H5

Where Cargo, the Patrol Helicopter, Chinook and the Travelling Vendor are. Only on servers that share map markers.

!help · !help timer[RustTrackerBro] !pop !time !wipe …

The list of commands; !help timer — a short hint for one command.

Good to know

  • If the server blocks messages that start with «!», use a dash: -pop, -time, -timer 12. The bot then answers with dash examples.
  • Russian words work too: !онлайн, !время, !вайп, !крафт, !рейд, !таймер.
  • Official Facepunch servers stopped sharing map markers after a Rust update — there !crate, !oil, !bradley, !cargo, !heli, !chinook and !vendor answer «Server map isn't available to Rust+». All other commands work on any server.
  • The same command — at most once every 10 seconds; all commands together — up to 12 answers a minute per team.
  • Commands can be turned off in the bot: 🔗 Rust+ → your server → 🔔 Notifications: bot & game → 💬 In-game chat commands.
