Rust+ chat commands

Anyone in your team types a command in game chat — the bot answers right there, tagged [RustTrackerBro]. Rust+ must be connected by at least one teammate in the bot.

CommandWhat the bot answers
!pop[RustTrackerBro] Online 87, queue 4

Server online and queue.

!time[RustTrackerBro] Time 18:40, night in ~12 min

In-game time and how long until night or morning.

!wipe[RustTrackerBro] Wiped 3 d 4 h ago · Global wipe in 15 d 22 h

Time since the wipe, the next wipe if the server wipes weekly, and the global wipe.

!timer 12[RustTrackerBro] ⏱ Timer: 12 min — I'll remind 1 min before and at zero

A timer for the crate or anything else: !timer 12:30, !timer crate, !timer heli, !timer bradley; !timer — active timers, !timer stop — reset the last one.

!decay stone 300[RustTrackerBro] Stone 300 HP — falls in about 3 h, reminder 10 min before

Look at an enemy wall with a hammer, type its material and HP — the bot reminds in chat and in the bot when it's about to fall. From Bro+.

!decay[RustTrackerBro] Without upkeep it decays in about: … stone 5 h …

How long walls of each material decay without upkeep.

!upkeep[RustTrackerBro] Cupboards: Home 31 h

Upkeep left on your team's cupboards (a storage monitor on the cupboard).

!craft c4[RustTrackerBro] C4 x1: 20 explosives, 5 cloth, 2 tech trash · workbench 3

Craft cost, workbench and time; with gunpowder or explosives — raw sulfur and charcoal.

!recycle tech trash 5[RustTrackerBro] Recycle Tech Trash x5: …

What you get from a recycler and in a safe zone.

!raid stone wall 2[RustTrackerBro] Stone Wall x2: … Cheapest: … sulfur

How many C4, rockets, satchels or explosive ammo — and the cheapest by sulfur.

!crate[RustTrackerBro] Crates at G12, K7

Where the locked crates are now.

!oil[RustTrackerBro] Oil rig activated 3 min ago

Whether a Chinook with scientists is at the oil rig now.

!bradley[RustTrackerBro] Bradley destroyed 4 min ago

When Bradley was destroyed.

!cargo · !heli · !chinook · !vendor[RustTrackerBro] Cargo at H5

Where Cargo, the patrol helicopter, Chinook and the travelling vendor are — on servers that share map markers.

!help · !help timer[RustTrackerBro] !pop !time !wipe …

The list of commands; !help timer — a short hint for one command.

Good to know

  • If the server blocks messages that start with «!», use a dash: -pop, -time, -timer 12. The bot then answers with dash examples.
  • Russian words work too: !онлайн, !время, !вайп, !крафт, !рейд, !таймер.
  • The same command — at most once every 10 seconds; all commands together — up to 12 answers a minute per team.
  • Commands can be turned off in the bot: 🔗 Rust+ → 🔔 Notifications: bot & game → 💬 In-game chat commands.
