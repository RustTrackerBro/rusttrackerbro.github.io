Questions

Is it free?

Yes. The Free tier is free with no time limit: 1 player, 1 server and Rust+. More players, history and analytics are in Bro+ and Bro Max, and newcomers get a Bro+ trial.

Discord or Telegram?

Both. The bot is the same in Discord and Telegram, and you can link the accounts — your plan and settings become shared.

Is it safe to connect Rust+?

You sign in through Steam on Facepunch's official page — the bot never sees your password. Tokens are stored encrypted, and you can delete everything with one button.

How fast do alerts arrive?

On Free — within 1–3 minutes, on Bro+ — in 30–60 seconds, on Bro Max — in about 30 seconds.

Do I need to install anything?

No: the bot works in Discord and Telegram. For Rust+ you need a computer with Chrome once — connecting takes about 2 minutes.
