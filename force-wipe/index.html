What gets wiped

  • The map, all buildings, items and resources — on every server.
  • Blueprints — by server rules: many servers wipe them too; sometimes Facepunch makes a blueprint wipe mandatory.
  • The game updates at the same time — Steam downloads it before you can join.
When is the next Rust force wipe?

On the first Thursday of the month at around 2 pm New York time. The exact countdown is at the top of this page.

What time is the force wipe in New York?

In New York it is at 14:00 all year round — see the table for each date.

Do all servers wipe?

Yes — the global wipe is mandatory for every server because of the monthly update. Many servers also wipe weekly or every two weeks.
