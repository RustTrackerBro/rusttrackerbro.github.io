Gene planner

Enter your clones' genes — get what to plant in the centre of a large planter and around it to breed a god clone (3 G + 3 Y in any order), with exact odds and a step-by-step plan.

Your clones

Tap the letters or paste genes: GGYYXH, GYGYHH x3 (x3 — how many of these clones).

G growth · Y yield · H hardiness — good (weight 0.6). W water · X empty — bad (weight 1.0).

Each slot is decided separately: the neighbours' weights for each gene add up, and the winner replaces the centre's gene only if its sum is strictly higher than the weight of the centre's gene. A tie between neighbours is a coin flip.
